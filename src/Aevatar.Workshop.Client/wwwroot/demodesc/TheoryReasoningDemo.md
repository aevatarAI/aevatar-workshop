## Reasoning Methods Explained

### Deductive Reasoning
- **Process**: Derive logical conclusions from established premises
- **Example**: From "All φ-sequences converge" and "Fibonacci ratios form φ-sequence" → "Fibonacci ratios converge"
- **Strength**: Guaranteed logical validity
- **Application**: Extending existing theory systems

### Inductive Reasoning
- **Process**: Identify patterns in specific examples to form general principles
- **Example**: Observing φ appears in nature → General principle about φ-based structures
- **Strength**: Discovery of new patterns
- **Application**: Finding universal mathematical laws

### Abductive Reasoning
- **Process**: Infer the most likely explanation for observed phenomena
- **Example**: Entropy always increases → Best explanation: No-11 constraint in binary encoding
- **Strength**: Explanatory power
- **Application**: Understanding why mathematical relationships exist

### Analogical Reasoning
- **Process**: Transfer structural patterns between different domains
- **Example**: Fibonacci in biology → φ-optimization in information theory
- **Strength**: Cross-domain insight
- **Application**: Unifying seemingly unrelated mathematical areas
